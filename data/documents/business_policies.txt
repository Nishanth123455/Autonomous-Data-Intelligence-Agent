BUSINESS ANALYTICS AND SALES POLICIES

1. Regional Sales Review Policy

Regional sales performance should be reviewed quarterly.
A regional revenue decline greater than 15 percent compared with
the previous quarter should trigger a management review.

The review should examine:
- Order volume
- Product mix
- Customer segments
- Pricing
- Discounts
- Operational issues

A decline in one region should not automatically be interpreted
as evidence of a single root cause. Additional analysis should be
performed before causal conclusions are made.

2. Product Performance Policy

Products showing a revenue decline greater than 20 percent
quarter-over-quarter should be reviewed by the sales team.

The review should examine:
- Sales volume
- Average selling price
- Discount levels
- Customer demand
- Regional distribution

High-revenue products should receive priority during performance
reviews because changes in these products can have a substantial
effect on total company revenue.

3. Discount Policy

Discounts should be monitored when analyzing changes in revenue
and profitability.

Increasing discounts do not automatically imply that discounts
caused a revenue decline. Analysts should examine both the change
in discount levels and the corresponding change in sales volume
before making conclusions.

4. Data Analysis Policy

Analytical conclusions must be supported by observed data.

Analysts should distinguish between:
- Descriptive findings
- Statistical associations
- Predictions
- Causal conclusions

A correlation or observed contribution should not automatically
be described as causation.

5. Management Recommendation Policy

Recommendations should be based on measurable evidence.

When evidence is insufficient to identify a root cause, the
recommendation should state the uncertainty and propose additional
analysis rather than presenting an unsupported conclusion as fact.

6. Quarterly Performance Policy

Quarterly performance should be compared with previous quarters
and, where possible, historical periods.

A significant decline should be investigated across:
- Regions
- Products
- Categories
- Customer segments
- Order volume
- Pricing
- Discounts
- Profitability
